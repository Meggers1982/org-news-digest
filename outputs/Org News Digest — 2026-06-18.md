### KFF (Kaiser Family Foundation)
*Health Policy & Cost Research*

**New polling data on public trust in health information, social media, and AI use**
Published: 2026-06-17 | Source: KFF Publications | [Dashboard](https://www.kff.org/public-opinion/kff-polling-on-health-information-and-trust/) | [Poll findings](https://www.kff.org/public-opinion/kff-tracking-poll-on-health-information-and-trust-use-of-social-media-and-ai-for-health-information-and-advice/)

KFF released an interactive dashboard and accompanying poll tracking public trust in health information sources, including attitudes toward vaccines and use of social media and AI for health advice. Key finding: approximately 30% of adults use social media for health information at least monthly, driven by community connection and need for immediate answers. Slim majorities of social media users are confident they can distinguish accurate information, but relatively few verify what they receive.

**Flag:** 🟢 Note only
Relevant for understanding how older adults and caregivers may be receiving health information; no direct policy implication but useful context for patient education and misinformation trends.

**Uninsured population grew in 2024 for first time since 2019**
Published: 2026-06-16 | Source: KFF Publications | [Link](https://www.kff.org/uninsured/key-facts-about-the-uninsured-population/)

KFF analysis of American Community Survey data shows the number and share of uninsured individuals increased in 2024 — the first rise since 2019. The brief examines characteristics of the uninsured population and summarizes access and financial implications of coverage gaps.

**Flag:** 🟡 Monitor closely
Coverage erosion affects LTC workforce (many aides are uninsured or underinsured) and family caregivers. Watch for state-level data and any connection to Medicaid unwinding trends.

**State policy tracker: immigrant access to services and enforcement activity**
Published: 2026-06-16 | Source: KFF Publications | [Link](https://www.kff.org/racial-equity-and-health-policy/recent-state-actions-related-to-immigrants-access-to-services-and-immigration-enforcement/)

KFF summarizes recent and proposed state actions from 2025–2026 legislative sessions affecting immigrant access to state-funded health coverage and other services, as well as immigration enforcement cooperation. Covers both restrictive and protective policy directions.

**Flag:** 🟡 Monitor closely
Direct implications for LTC workforce availability and for immigrant elders' access to services. Useful reference for tracking state-by-state policy divergence.

Published: 2026-06-17 | Source: RAND Research | [Link](https://www.rand.org/pubs/external_publications/EP71340.html)
