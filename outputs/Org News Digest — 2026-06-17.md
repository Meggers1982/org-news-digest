### **Medicaid State Directed Payments Near $100B Annually; New Federal Limits Will Reduce State Funding**
Published: 2026-06-15 | Source: KFF Publications | [Link](https://www.kff.org/medicaid/federal-medicaid-spending-through-state-directed-payments-nears-100-billion-annually-across-41-states-with-new-limits-set-to-reduce-funding-to-states/)

KFF estimates that 40 states plus DC currently receive $93 billion annually in federal Medicaid spending through state directed payments (SDPs), with California ($10.6B), Texas ($6.3B), North Carolina ($5.2B), and Illinois ($5.1B) receiving the largest amounts. These payments are now subject to new caps under the 2025 reconciliation law, which reduced federal Medicaid spending by an estimated $911 billion over 2025–2034.

**Flag:** 🔴 Action required  
SDP reductions will directly affect Medicaid-funded long-term care providers, particularly nursing facilities and HCBS programs relying on supplemental payments. States with large LTC Medicaid populations face near-term rate restructuring.
