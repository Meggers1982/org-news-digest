# Press Digest Preferences

Edit this file directly on GitHub to tune what shows up in the press-highlights section of the dashboard.
Changes take effect the next run automatically — no code changes needed.

---

## See more of
- Clinical trial results, especially mental health, oncology, and women's health
- FDA approvals and regulatory decisions for consumer health products
- Research studies, polls, and surveys with surprising or counterintuitive findings
- Wellness and fitness brand expansions into new categories or markets
- Public health findings with broad consumer relevance
- Food and beverage launches, trends, and brand news — including new products, restaurant expansions, ingredient trends, and consumer behavior shifts
- Lifestyle topics: home, travel, wellness routines, personal finance habits, relationships
- Health and nutrition science, including diet trends, supplements, and functional foods
- Fitness industry news: new concepts, equipment, digital fitness platforms, and sports nutrition
- Consumer brand moments: collabs, rebrands, cultural tie-ins, limited editions

## See less of
- Beauty and cosmetics product launches (unless backed by clinical research)
- Entertainment news unless it's a genuinely unexpected collaboration or cultural shift
- Travel deals and promotional announcements
- B2B food/beverage supply chain or ingredient sourcing stories with no consumer angle

## Volume
- Always aim for at least 15 stories. On slow news days, lower the bar rather than send a short digest.
- It's fine to include mildly interesting stories — a noteworthy brand launch, a relevant survey, a product expansion — even if they're not groundbreaking.
- Never send fewer than 10 stories. If strong picks are scarce, fill with the best available from health, food, lifestyle, and consumer categories.

---

## Notes
- Stories seen in the last 7 days are automatically skipped (deduplication is handled for you)
- You can be as specific or general as you like — e.g. "always include anything about GLP-1 drugs"
- Add or remove bullet points freely; the AI reads this file as plain instructions
